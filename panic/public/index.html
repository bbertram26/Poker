The Devastating Truth About Gambling

💔

Destroys Families

Gambling addiction tears apart marriages, ruins relationships, and leaves children without the security they need. Over 1 million children are affected by parental gambling problems each year, creating lasting trauma and instability.

💰

Financial Ruin

Problem gamblers lose an average of $55,000 annually. Many lose their homes, life savings, and retirement funds, leaving families in devastating debt that can take decades to recover from.

🧠

Mental Health Crisis

Gambling addiction leads to depression, anxiety, and suicide rates 15 times higher than the general population. It changes brain chemistry similar to drug addiction, creating a cycle of despair.

⚰️

Spiritual Death

Gambling promotes greed, destroys contentment, and leads people away from God's plan for their lives. It violates biblical principles of stewardship and trust in God's provision.

Protecting Our Children

Why Children Must Be Protected from Gambling

  • Developing Brains: Children's brains are still developing until age 25, making them extremely vulnerable to addiction and poor decision-making patterns.
  • Normalized Behavior: Early exposure to gambling normalizes risky behavior and creates unhealthy attitudes toward money and chance.
  • Academic Failure: Youth who gamble are more likely to have poor grades, behavioral problems, and drop out of school entirely.
  • Criminal Activity: Teen gambling often leads to stealing money to fund the habit, creating a pathway to more serious crimes.
  • Lifetime Addiction: The earlier someone starts gambling, the more likely they are to develop a serious, life-destroying problem as an adult.

Biblical Wisdom

"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it." — Proverbs 22:6

As Christians, we have a sacred responsibility to protect children from harmful influences and guide them toward righteousness and godly character.
