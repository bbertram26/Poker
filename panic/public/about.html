A Heart for the Broken

God's Calling

Holliday Bertram's journey into anti-gambling ministry began with a divine burden placed on her heart by the Lord. As a devoted follower of Jesus Christ, she witnessed firsthand the devastating effects of gambling addiction in her community and felt God's unmistakable call to stand against this destructive force.

A Biblical Foundation

Rooted deeply in Scripture, Holliday's ministry approach is built on the solid foundation of God's Word. She believes that true freedom from gambling addiction comes not through worldly methods alone, but through the transforming power of the Gospel and a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

"If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed." - John 8:36

The Burden for Families

Having seen countless families torn apart by gambling addiction, Holliday is particularly passionate about protecting children and strengthening family units. She firmly believes that gambling not only destroys individuals but attacks the very foundation of society - the family - which God designed as the cornerstone of human civilization.

Biblical Truth About Gambling

Holliday's ministry is built on the clear biblical principles that expose gambling as contrary to God's design for human flourishing:

Stewardship

"Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful." - 1 Corinthians 4:2

God calls us to be faithful stewards of the resources He provides, not to risk them in games of chance.

Contentment

"But godliness with contentment is great gain." - 1 Timothy 6:6

Gambling feeds on discontentment and the desire for quick wealth, which God warns against.

Trust in God

"But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus." - Philippians 4:19

God promises to provide for our needs, eliminating any justification for gambling as a solution to financial problems.

Love of Money

"For the love of money is the root of all evil." - 1 Timothy 6:10

Gambling cultivates the love of money and leads people away from faith and into many sorrows.
